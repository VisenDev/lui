Lighting speed measurements, 2026-11-22

Commands: make benchmark-wayland / make benchmark-wayland-uncapped
SBCL 2.6.9; Ryzen 7 7800X3D; Radeon RX 7800 XT; Wayland.
Logical 720x520, physical 1080x780; 65 equally weighted cases;
260 warmup frames, 1300 measured frames (20 per case).

Baseline rendering: e681147, with the two-decimal layout label.
Uncapped baseline additionally uses the new FPS toggle, nullable frame cap,
and EGL swap-interval control; renderer/font optimizations are excluded.
Optimized rendering: glyph identity cache, direct checkerboard triangles,
and cached font-unit advances/kerning. Same viewport and sample schedule.

ACTIVE = construction/layout + clear + painting through glFinish.
Build/layout/GPU-finish columns are nested, not additive to ACTIVE.
RESULT includes presentation and event processing. Uncapped runs request
continuous frames and EGL interval zero. Actual display scanout is not FPS.
Allocation counters are SBCL process-wide bytes; foreign allocations excluded.
Timings vary by run and are evidence for this device, not guarantees.

Uncapped matched comparison: 1.700 to 0.796 ms/frame (588 to 1256 FPS),
active 1.652 to 0.743 ms/frame, allocation 630601 to 355790 bytes/frame.
Additional optimized uncapped runs: 0.773 and 0.770 ms/frame,
active 0.720 and 0.720 ms/frame. Normal presentation remains paced.
